School of Radiologic Technology

The School of Radiologic Technology at Southland College is dedicated to preparing future healthcare professionals through quality education and hands-on training in medical imaging.

The four-year Bachelor of Science in Radiologic Technology program combines classroom instruction, laboratory activities, and supervised clinical experience. Students gain essential knowledge in anatomy, radiation physics, imaging procedures, radiation protection, and patient care.

Authorized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) to operate since 2017, the school aims to develop competent, compassionate, and responsible radiologic technologists who are prepared to serve in hospitals, diagnostic centers, and other healthcare institutions.

Radiologic Technology History

  1. 1 1895: Wilhelm Röntgen discovers X-rays.
  2. 2 1913: The Coolidge tube makes X-ray images clearer and more reliable.
  3. 3 1920s: Radiologic technology becomes a recognized profession.
  4. 4 1950s-60s: Fluoroscopy, nuclear medicine, and ultrasound emerge.
  5. 5 1970s: CT scanning gives doctors cross-sectional images of the body.
  6. 6 1980s: MRI enters clinical use.
  7. 7 1990s-2000s: Digital imaging and PACS replace film.
  8. 8 Today: 3D imaging, AI-assisted diagnosis, and low-dose techniques.

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Radiologic Technology (BSRT) degree program is a four-year program that prepares students to become skilled radiologic technologists. The program covers a wide range of topics in radiation science and imaging technology, including conventional radiography, computed tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), nuclear medicine, ultrasonography, and radiation therapy. Graduates of the program will be able to use both ionizing and non-ionizing radiation modalities to perform a variety of diagnostic and therapeutic medical procedures. They will also be equipped with the knowledge and skills to ensure the safe and effective use of radiation in the healthcare setting.

The BSRT program is a great choice for students who are interested in a career in healthcare and who are passionate about using technology to help people. The program offers a challenging but rewarding curriculum that will prepare students for a successful career in radiologic technology.

Upon graduation, BSRT students will be eligible to take the Radiologic Technologist Licensure Examination (RTLE). Once they pass the RTLE, they will be able to practice as Registered Radiologic Technologist in the Philippines.

Career

A degree in Radiologic Technology opens doors in hospitals, diagnostic centers, and beyond. After passing the licensure exam (given by the PRC in the Philippines), graduates can pursue roles such as:

Clinical Roles

  • Radiologic Technologist (X-ray)
  • CT Scan Technologist
  • MRI Technologist
  • Ultrasound Technologist / Sonographer
  • Mammography Technologist
  • Fluoroscopy and Special Procedures Technologist
  • Nuclear Medicine Technologist
  • Radiation Therapy Technologist

Specialized and Advanced Roles

  • Cardiac Catheterization Lab Technologist
  • Bone Densitometry (DEXA) Technologist
  • PACS Administrator / Imaging Informatics Specialist
  • Applications Specialist (for imaging equipment companies)

Leadership and Academic Roles

  • Radiology Department Supervisor or Manager
  • Quality Assurance and Radiation Safety Officer
  • Clinical Instructor or Professor
  • Researcher
